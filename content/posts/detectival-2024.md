#### Dates for Detectival 2024 And Where to Buy Tickets.

Good News, the dates for Detectival 2024 have finally been released to the public, although later than normal and do conflict with another metal detecting rally, the tickets can be ordered/reserved at [DETECTIVAL.COM](http://www.detectival.com)

Looking like it will be in north hertfordshire of the uk, with 1000 acres of land to detect.

As always this will attract detectorists and manufacturers from around the globe.
