## RNB Power X Review

<strong>RNB Power X Battery Pack Review:</strong> A Versatile Powerhouse

![minelab manticore with the rnb power x has a better detecting time than just the standard battery in low frequency detecting modes](/img/powerx_review1.webp)

The *RNB Power X battery pack* is quickly become a favourite among metal detectorists, and for good reason. Its impressive capacity and lightweight design make it an ideal companion for long detecting sessions, especially when paired with power-hungry machines like the Minelab Manticore. Allowing in the <strong>*high power*</strong> detecting modes of the Minelab Manticore around 24 hours of use before a re-charge, and much much longer with the Equinox and X-Terra detectors.

### *Excellent Performance* with the Minelab Manticore &amp; Other Detectors

<strong>Extended Detecting Sessions:</strong> The Power X's 10,000 mAh capacity provides ample power to keep the Manticore and other USB-charged detectors running for hours on end. This translates to *longer hunting trips* without the worry of running out of battery.

*No Performance Drop-Off*: Even with the Manticore's demanding power requirements, the Power X maintains a consistent output, ensuring no noticeable drop in detector performance throughout the day. The Detector will use the POWER X Battery before using its own power.

<strong>Lightweight and Convenient:</strong> Weighing only 234 grams, the Power X adds minimal weight to your setup, making it comfortable to carry even during extended hunts. Its compact size and easy-to-use mounting system make it a breeze to attach and detach from your detector.

![when and how to use a POWER X Battery on a metal detector](/img/rnb-Power-x1.webp)

<strong>High-Power Output Modes:</strong> The Power X is particularly beneficial when using the Manticore in its high-power output low-frequency modes. It ensures you can utilise these powerful modes for extended periods without worrying about running out of battery, maximising your chances of finding valuable targets.

*Improved Balance*: The Power X also acts as a small counterweight, helping to balance out the weight of the search coil and improve overall handling and comfort.

*Versatility*: The Power X is NOT only suited to the Minelab Manticore but also compatible with other metal detectors that charge via USB.

*Durable Construction*: The Power X is built to withstand the rigours of regular use, ensuring long-lasting performance. Although *NOT waterproof* the Power X is shower proof and has rubber bungs to close in the electrical connections.
