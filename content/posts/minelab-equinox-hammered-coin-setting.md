## minelab equinox settings for hammered coins

highly sought after for most people metal detecting are hammered coins.

these very thin and usually tiny coins can be notoriously difficult to find

![Small hammered silver coin held on a fingertip in front of grass](/img/P1060612_web.webp "A tiny, thin hammered coin like this is easy to miss")

that is unless they are laid dead flat and then give off a great tone on the metal detector.

Most people will opt for one setting like beach to land which to be fair will detect anything so long as it is under the coil.

This time just to show that which mode you use as long as it is set up correctly for where you are searching will find these small hammered coins.

we set up in "Park 1 search mode" on the equinox 800 and using the 15 inch minelab elliptical coil.

![Detectorist with a Minelab Equinox and spade standing in a ploughed field](/img/GH010590_web.webp "Searching a field in Park 1 mode with the 15 inch elliptical coil")

The advantage of NOT using the beach to land setting is that if you detect under electric pylons or near to an electric fence the you have the ability to switch to a single frequency to loose the pulsing of the electrical interference.

![Detectorist crouching to dig a signal in a dry field with the Equinox coil beside him](/img/GH010591_web.webp "small: Digging a small signal: hammered coins often lie dead flat")
