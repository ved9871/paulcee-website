[![seahunter settings](/img/Web-seahunter-settings.webp "small")](https://www.youtube.com/subscription_center?add_user=flash1968)Its not too often I'll write a "how to piece" on setting up a [metal detector](/beginners-guide-to-metal-detecting/) as everyone has their own preferences, although everytime i do get asked when im out people often comment "You use Discrimination?"

Well when i use the either the Garrett Seahunter MkII or the Garrett ATX, yes they both have a somewhat crude form of discrimination even though they are [Pulse Induction metal Detectors](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Metal_detector).

The Seahunter has the discrimination knob on the rear of the control panel

![garrett pi detector](/img/seahunter-settings.webp "Garrett Seahunter mk2 settings")

Along with the ON/Off Control and also the Threshold knob.

Usually for Detecting on "Wet" Sand I have the discrimination set at "0" and the threshold at just above audible so i can hear the tone, it is a tone and not a hum as with many detectors including my CTX3030.

![Garrett seahunter threshold](/img/a-5.webp "Garrett seahunter threshold")

This is my setting for the threshold

![Garrett seahunter settings For Beach Metal Detecting](/img/a-8.webp "Garrett Seahunter Mk 2 Settings")

### My Discrimination Setting for "Wet" Sand

Now the Setting for Discrimination on the Dry Sand. I use this mainly to not pick up bottle tops and Bobby/Hair pins.

It is i would say 80% ok but yes i do still did bottle tops but not as many.. I Do not turn any higher and try to loose ring pulls or tabs as this also in the range of some fine jewellery, i.e. gold... and even on my [CTX 3030](/ctx3030/) I do not discriminate out those, I did everything on the beach if it sounds good, but with a PI detector it will pick up everything..

![seahunter mk2 setup](/img/a-6.webp "Garrett Seahunter Mk 2 set up")

I Have the dial at About 6 or just slightly under... this does also Affect the depth that this will now Seek the goodies, I've found it reduces to about 6"-7" maximum, but on the dry sand where losses are usually fresh this isn't really too much of an issue..

![discrete discrimination on the Garrett Seahunter](/img/a-7.webp "discrete discrimination on the Garrett Seahunter")

Now the Discreet Discrimination setting, is pretty handy as you hear the threshold but without all the background noise of the standard setting.

![GARRETT DISCRIMINATION](/img/a-9.webp "Garrett Seahunter Discrimination")

But on the wet sand i have this on standard as to hear everything thats going on, usually on the beaches i detect its very noisy as there littered with iron.

![pulse induction cleethorpes](/img/a-10.webp "Cleethorpes Beach Metal Detecting")

![garrett seahunter mk 2 uk beach detecting settings](/img/a-11.webp "Cleethorpes Beach Metal Detecting")

So there you have it, these are the best setting for me when coin shooting with the Garrett Seahunter 2. you may find them useful, or they may not work for you and where you detect. At the end of the day there arn't many controls so just have a twiddle..

Feel Free to comment if they work for you or not..
