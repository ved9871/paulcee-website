### Essential Upgrades for the Minelab Manticore

The Minelab Manticore is already a beast of a machine right out of the box. But seasoned detectorists know that a few key modifications can turn a great day of hunting into a perfect one.

If you are looking to streamline your setup for better portability and audio freedom, we are spotlighting two specific accessories today: the GOG Removable Folding Handle and the GOG Wireless Phones.

![Minelab Manticore metal detector featuring GOG folding removable handle and GOG wireless headphones with WM09 module on the beach.](/img/20251207_082004.webp)

### 1. The GOG Manticore Removable Folding Handle

As shown in the top half of the image, this accessory addresses one of the few gripes travellers have with the stock setup: packability.

The standard fixed handle can make fitting your detector into a backpack or travel case a challenge. The **GOG Removable Folding Handle** changes the game by introducing a robust hinge mechanism that **easily enables you to totally remove** the control handle.

<strong>Compact Travel:</strong> By allowing the handle to fold or detach, you can break your Manticore down to a fraction of its assembled size.

<strong>Protection:</strong> Folding the unit often allows you to tuck the control box safely inward, protecting that precious screen during transport.

<strong>Rugged Design:</strong> Built to withstand the torque and pressure of a long day swinging on the beach or in the field.

### 2. GOG Phones (WM09 Wireless Integration)

Wireless audio is a must for modern detecting, but nobody likes dealing with dongles dangling from their pockets.

**Highlighted at the bottom of the image**, the GOG Phones offer a seamless solution. These are custom-designed to incorporate the Minelab WM09 Wireless Module directly.

**Cable-Free Detecting**: Say goodbye to getting snagged on branches. The "GOG Phones" branding on the ear cup represents true wireless freedom.

**Low Latency**: Because they utilize the <strong>[WM09 module](/detecting-accessories/)</strong>, you get the lightning-fast signal response Minelab is famous for—vital for hearing those faint, deep targets.

<strong>Integrated Comfort:</strong> Instead of clipping a module to your shirt or belt, it is integrated into the headset itself, keeping your setup clean and professional.
