## Minelab Equinox small hammered coins

Metal detecting on Stubble with the [Minelab Equinox](/minelab-equinox/) today we venture to the Midlands in England on another Rally.

Set up the Equinox in Field 1 and 50 tones manual Ground balance ( ground Balance varied between 28 - 38) you could set the Ground Balance to tracking in such a circumstance. recovery speed 3 and iron Bias set at 2 generally picking out buttons and small pieces of lead.

no real areas to call hot spots were found but once a decent target was found then an area of about 10 sq ft was gridded..
