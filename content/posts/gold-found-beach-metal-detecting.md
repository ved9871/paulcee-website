### More gold found with the Minelab Equinox 800

### metal detecting the beach at Cleethorpes.

Early morning on the beach today as high tide was so early. Setting the Equinox into beach mode 2 and sensitivity to 23, 5 tones are my personal favourite and manual ground balance. Iron Bias set to 5 recovery speed set to 6, and iron discrimination upto 2 on the tone breaks and then run in all metal mode as this beach has lots of shallow iron so these settings help a lot.

The gold came in at number 9-10 on the middle of the slope.
