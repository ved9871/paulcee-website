## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Is the Minelab Vanquish 360 fully waterproof?

\*\*Yes.\*\* The Minelab Vanquish 360 has an \*\*IP68 rating\*\*, making it completely waterproof and submersible up to \*\*5 metres (16 ft)\*\*. You can safely detect in heavy rain, rivers, shallow surf, and wet saltwater beaches without worrying about water damage to the control box or the included V10X coil.

### What is the best search mode on the Vanquish 360 for UK farmland and beaches?

\* \*\*For UK farmland and pasture:\*\* Use \*\*All Metal\*\* mode. It accepts all Target IDs (\`-19\` to \`99\`), ensuring you do not accidentally filter out tiny medieval hammered silver coins or thin gold items that sit low on the conductivity scale.

\* \*\*For wet sand and beaches:\*\* Use \*\*Beach\*\* mode. It uses Minelab's Multi-IQ processing to eliminate false signals caused by conductive salt water while keeping the full non-ferrous scale (\`1\` to \`99\`) active.

### Does the Minelab Vanquish 360 need to be ground balanced manually?

\*\*No.\*\* Ground balancing is completely automatic on the Vanquish 360. Because it runs simultaneous multi-frequency \*\*Multi-IQ technology\*\*, the detector continuously tracks and adapts to changing soil mineralisation—from inland pasture to wet salt sand—without requiring manual pumping or ground-balance adjustments.

### How long does the Vanquish 360 battery last, and how do you charge it?

The built-in rechargeable lithium-ion battery delivers \*\*up to 10 hours of continuous detecting\*\* on a full charge. You recharge it using the supplied \*\*magnetic USB charging cable\*\* plugged into any standard USB wall adapter or portable power bank.

### Can you use wireless headphones with the Minelab Vanquish 360?

\*\*No.\*\* The Vanquish 360 does not support wireless Bluetooth headphones. It features a built-in external loudspeaker and a \*\*wired 3.5 mm (⅛") headphone socket\*\*. If you want native wireless audio out of the box, look at the \*\*Minelab Vanquish 460 or 560\*\*.{{product key="vanquish-560"}}

### Why does my Vanquish 360 chatter or give random numbers when nothing is under the coil?

Random chatter is almost always caused by \*\*Electromagnetic Interference (EMI)\*\* from overhead power lines, electric fences, mobile phones, or \*\*Sensitivity set too high\*\*. Hold the coil still in the air and restart the detector to trigger a fresh \*\*Automatic Noise Cancel\*\*. If it still chatters, reduce the Sensitivity from level 5 down to \*\*level 3 or 4\*\*.

### Can you swap coils on the Minelab Vanquish 360?

\*\*Yes.\*\* While the detector comes standard with the versatile \*\*V10X 10" × 7" Double-D elliptical coil\*\*, it is compatible with optional Minelab Equinox and Coiltek coils such as the smaller \*\*V8X coil\*\* for tight, iron-infested sites or larger \*\*15" × 12" DD coils\*\* for maximum ground coverage and depth on open fields.
