## Pwr-Nox Power Pack for Minelab Equinox Detectors

Double the battery life and detecting time of your Minelab Equinox with the Pwr-Nox Power Pack.

![Pwr-Nox power pack clipped to the upper stem of an Equinox shaft](/img/pwr-nox002a_ig2l0m52.webp "It clips to the upper stem beside the arm cup")

This lightweight, compact unit fits neatly to the upper stem via the arm cup screw and its front clip. Connect to the control Equinox box using the standard Equinox USB charge cable.

![Pwr-Nox USB power pack at an angle showing the clip on its back](/img/pwr-nox003a.webp "It plugs into the control box with the standard USB lead")

The fully charged power pack has the same capacity as the standard Equinox rechargeable battery, effectively doubling your time detecting without needing to charge.

![Close-up of the Pwr-Nox power pack fixed with the arm cup screw and clip](/img/pwr-nox006a.webp "Fixed in place with the arm cup screw and front clip")

The unit can be recharged with the included USB to Micro-USB charge lead, taking power from a mains adaptor, vehicle output or almost any USB enabled power source.
