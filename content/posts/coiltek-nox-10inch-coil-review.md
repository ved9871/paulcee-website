## Test and REVIEW of the COILTEK NOX 10x5 inch coil.

This COILTEK Coil is IDEAL for metal detector searching in very trashy areas.

We Came across some serious beach errosion and knew straight away the the Coiltek Nox Coil 10x5 would be ideal for this area.

The Coiltek Coils will work with the EQUINOX 700 and 900 and also the Minelab x-terra Pro

The Minelab Equinox paired with the Coiltek 10x5 NOX Coil is going to be an unrivaled power for searching trashy areas, rockpools and inbetween boulders.

You can also keep on the Coiltek Coil for dry sand searching as it still has the 10 inch length to cover ground but also using the 5 inch width the easily identify targets that are close together.
