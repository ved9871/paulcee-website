## Coiltek Nox 10x5 Search Coil UK

For the [Minelab Equinox](/minelab-equinox/). This Coiltek Coil is ideal for searching trashy areas littered with lots of targets, and superb for beach detecting where there are alot of rocks and areas where a larger search coil would struggle to separate the good finds from the underlying junk.
