## Carry case for Metal detectors

Also Suitable for the Minelab Equinox 900/700 and the Minelab X-terra Pro and GPX 6000, along with other brand metal detectors which collapse.

such as Deus 1 and deus 2, nokta legend and nokta simplex.

Made from 600D water resistant Fabric, fitted with a 3 sided zipper to allow wide opening, and Velcro straps inside to secure your detector

### Large Carry case for Minelab Manticore and search coils

![Open CMD case holding a Minelab Manticore, search coils and spare lower shafts](/img/cmd-case_manti_coils.webp "Room for the Manticore, extra coils and spare shafts")

This CMD Large case will also carry extra search coils including the COILTEK NOX 18, plus spare lower shafts.

Manticore featured with the optional M15 and M8 search coils, Equinox 900 and COILTEK NOX 14x9 and Minelab 6"search coil.

![Minelab headphones lying on the front pocket of the black CMD Large carry case](/img/cmd-case_headphones.webp "The front pocket also takes headphones and a 15x12 coil")

The Front pocket will fit more accessories including 15x12 search coil, headphones,

charging leads, power pack, gloves or other stuff you carry about.

Pocket size 330mm (13 inch) x 400mm (15 3/4 inch)

The Front of the CMD Large case

is Fitted with MOLLE style fittings

which allow for fitting a water bottle holder, 1st aid kit and more

Along with the top edge carry handles there are also 2 over shoulder fully adjustable straps to use the carry case as a ruck sack,

so you can setup your detector where you park and carry all the needed coils, cables and shafts with you
