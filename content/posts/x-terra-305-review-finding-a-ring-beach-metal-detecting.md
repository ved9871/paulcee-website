Out on the dry sand with the Minelab E-Terra 305, a [basic metal detector](/minelab-manticore/) designed for the beginner or entry level detectorist. this is truly underated as an [entry level metal detector](/minelab-vanquish-40-series/), works really well on the dry sand and the slightly moist slope of the beach, a slow swing speed and used with no discrimination, helps to pick out targets amongst the trash, in this video i was using only 3 tones, in the previous video i used it in multl tones..
