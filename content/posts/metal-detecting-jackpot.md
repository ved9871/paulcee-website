## [http://bit.ly/2VcUX0q](http://bit.ly/2VcUX0q)hammered coins with a minelab equinox

What an awesome day metal detecting with the [Minelab Equinox 800 ](/minelab-equinox/)and 15 inch coil, fresh ploughed field

and the day just started off superb, setup the equinox in field 1 and 50 tones, manual ground balance recovery speed 3 and iron bias 2.. nice and steady swing speed and the hammered coins just kept coming every once in a while throughout the day..
