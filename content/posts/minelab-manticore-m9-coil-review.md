## Minelab Manticore M9 Search Coil Review

![a review of the M9 coil for the Minelab Manticore](/img/m9-review.webp)

### Best Selling

The Manticore M9 has quickly become the best-selling coil for the Minelab Manticore. Its impressive features and reliable performance have made it a popular choice among metal detectorists.

### Slim Profile

One notable feature of the Manticore M9 is its slim profile, which is particularly suited for water detecting. The streamlined design, weighing only 262 grams, allows for easy manoeuvrability in water, helping users efficiently locate targets even in challenging underwater environments.

### Performance

The Minelab Manticore M9 search coil excels in delivering outstanding performance. It offers exceptional depth detection and precise pinpointing, making it an excellent choice for both beginners and experienced metal detector enthusiasts. Its advanced technology ensures that even small items are accurately detected. Of course its a smaller coil than the stock coil, which means slightly less ground coverage, so an adjusted swing speed of the Manticore is recommended.

### Durability

Built to withstand rugged conditions, the M9 coil is highly durable. Its robust construction and high-quality materials make it resistant to damage from rough terrains with a[ replaceable scuff plate](https://www.crawfordsmd.com/metal-detecting-accessories/accessories-minelab/minelab-m9-skidplate), ensuring long-lasting use. Users can confidently take it on various treasure-hunting adventures without worrying about wear and tear.

Strong coil lugs mean NO NEED for 3D printed stiffeners which to be honest are a waste of time and your money.

### Ease of Use

The M9 search coil is Plug and Play, so just unscrew the connectors and change coils over, there is no need to do a factory reset or other major adjustment.

Its lightweight design (262 grams) reduces arm fatigue, allowing for extended periods of use without discomfort. The coil’s ergonomic structure make it great to users of all skill levels.

### Accuracy

One of the standout features of the Manticore M9 is its unparalleled accuracy. With advanced discrimination capabilities of the Minelab Manticore, it can effectively distinguish between valuable targets and unwanted trash. This level of precision enhances the overall metal detecting experience, increasing the likelihood of finding treasure.

### Versatility

The Minelab M9 search coil is versatile and adaptable to various environments, including beaches, fields, and forests. Its ability to perform well in different conditions makes it a reliable companion for diverse metal detecting activities.

### Stabiliser Feature

Using the stabiliser feature on the Minelab Manticore, you have the ability to increase the sensitivity slightly, which will provide slightly more depth. This enhancement makes the M9 coil even more effective in detecting buried treasures.

### Recovery Speed

Using a recovery speed of 5 or 6 will still maintain a good depth and sharp target tones with the M9 coil. This allows users to effectively detect targets in areas with high trash density without compromising on depth.
