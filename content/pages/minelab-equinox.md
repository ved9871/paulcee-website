## Minelab Equinox Series

<strong>From the 600 to the 900:</strong> Multi-IQ Technology for Every Detectorist

## The Multi-IQ Revolution

The Equinox series changed the game with **Multi-IQ Technology**. Before this, you had to choose between a VLF machine (good for coins) or a Pulse Induction machine (good for salt).

Multi-IQ transmits multiple frequencies simultaneously (e.g., 5, 10, 15, 20, 40 kHz). This allows the detector to see large silver coins deep down *and* tiny gold nuggets near the surface at the exact same time.

## Speed &amp; Versatility

The Equinox is defined by its **Recovery Speed**. It is incredibly fast, allowing it to pick out a good target masked by iron trash.

- <strong>Waterproof:</strong> Fully submersible (3m for 600/800; 5m for 700/900).
- <strong>All-Terrain:</strong> Pre-set modes for Park, Field, Beach, and Gold (800/900 only) make it "turn on and go."
- <strong>Lightweight:</strong> Modern carbon-fiber shafts (on the newer 700/900 models) make swinging effortless.

## Comparing the Range

Minelab currently offers the older generation (600/800) and the upgraded generation (700/900).

### 💡 Key Difference: 600/800 vs 700/900

The newer **700 and 900** models feature a greatly expanded Target ID range (0-99 vs the old 0-40). This "High Resolution" ID spreads out the conductive targets, making it much easier to distinguish a silver dime from a penny or a screw cap.

### ✅ The Pros

- <strong>Value for Money:</strong> The Equinox 600/700 offers flagship performance at a mid-range price.
- <strong>Weight:</strong> Much lighter than the CTX 3030, making it easier for all-day hunting.
- <strong>Beach Performance:</strong> The Multi-IQ tech dominates on wet saltwater sand where single-frequency machines fail.
- <strong>Future Proof:</strong> Software updatable via Minelab Update Utility (MUU).

### ⚠️ The Cons

- <strong>Build Quality (Older Models):</strong> The older 600/800 had issues with coil ear breakage (fixed in 700/900).
- <strong>Target Compression:</strong> The older 600/800 compresses IDs (0-40), making some trash signal similar to coins.
- <strong>No Visual Trace:</strong> Lacks the visual "Target Trace" screen found on the CTX 3030 and Manticore.

## Available Search Coils

<em>Note: EQX coils fit both the older (600/800) and newer (700/900) models.</em>

### Official Minelab Coils

<strong>EQX 06 (6-inch Round DD):</strong>\
The "Sniper." Absolute necessity for iron-infested home sites or trashy parks. Laser-like separation.

<strong>EQX 11 (11-inch Round DD):</strong>\
The Standard. Included with all machines. The best balance of weight, depth, and coverage.

<strong>EQX 15 (15x12-inch Elliptical DD):</strong>\
The depth beast. Covers massive ground on beaches and pastures. Digs deeper than the 11".

### Approved Coiltek Coils (NOX Series)

<strong>Coiltek 10x5" NOX:</strong>\
Widely considered the "best coil ever made" for the Equinox. It separates like the 6" but covers ground like a 10".

<strong>Coiltek 14x9" NOX:</strong>\
A lightweight alternative to the Minelab 15". Excellent for water hunting as it has less drag.

<strong>Coiltek 15" Round NOX:</strong>\
A large round coil offering significant depth and punch. Great for open fields where depth is king.

<strong>Coiltek 18" NOX:</strong>\
The largest coil available. Strictly for deep hoard hunting or clean beaches.
