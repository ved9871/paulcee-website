### 💡 Paul Cee’s Golden Rule

"A quiet machine is always more efficient than a noisy one. Never chase high sensitivity if it causes chatter—you’ll only end up missing the very targets you're looking for."

### 📊 Sensitivity

Higher sensitivity increases "reach," but amplifies ground noise. If the machine chatters, it **drowns out** the tiny whispers of deep targets.

### 🧲 Discrimination

This filters out "junk" like nails. Be careful: blocking iron can "mask" a silver coin if it's sitting right next to a rusty nail.
