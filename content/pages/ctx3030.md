## Minelab CTX 3030

The Master of Stability &amp; Identification

## FBS 2 Technology

<strong>Full Band Spectrum 2 (FBS 2)</strong> is the engine that drives the CTX 3030. Unlike single-frequency detectors that struggle in mineralized ground or saltwater, FBS 2 combines Minelab's multi-frequency transmission (1.5kHz – 100kHz) with advanced digital coil-to-detector communication.

The result is significantly better noise cancellation and signal analysis, allowing you to find deep silver coins even in iron-infested sites where other machines go silent.

## The Smart Screen &amp; Target Trace

The full-color LCD is more than just a display—it's a visual mapping tool.

- <strong>Target Trace:</strong> This features allows you to see multiple targets simultaneously. You can visually distinguish a good target (like a coin) sitting right next to a bad target (like a nail) based on their position on the FeCo (Ferrous/Conductive) graph.
- <strong>GPSi Mapping:</strong> The screen integrates GPS data, allowing you to create WayPoints and view "GeoTrails" so you can see exactly where you have already detected.

### 💻 The XChange 2 Advantage: Easy PC Programming

One of the CTX 3030's most distinct features is its full integration with the **Minelab XChange 2 PC application**.

Unlike newer machines where all settings must be tapped out on a small screen, the CTX 3030 allows you to:

- <strong>Design Custom Profiles on a Large Screen:</strong> Easily drag and drop discrimination patterns and Tone ID profiles using your mouse and keyboard.
- <strong>Manage Your Maps:</strong> View your "FindPoints" and "GeoTrails" on Google Maps to analyze where you found treasure.
- <strong>Upload/Download:</strong> Instantly transfer your custom settings to your detector via USB.

<em><strong>Note:</strong> This PC-based profile creation facility is **NOT available on the Minelab Manticore**, which relies solely on on-device programming.</em>

### ✅ The Pros

- <strong>PC Customization:</strong> Use XChange 2 to build complex programs easily on your computer rather than fiddling with buttons in the field.
- <strong>Best Visual ID:</strong> The Target Trace remains superior for identifying bottle caps versus coins.
- <strong>Stability:</strong> Extremely stable Target IDs at depth; numbers rarely jump on deep silver.
- <strong>GPS:</strong> The only high-end machine that logs your exact find locations.

### ⚠️ The Cons

- <strong>Weight:</strong> It is heavy. A detecting harness is often required for long sessions.
- <strong>Recovery Speed:</strong> Slower processor speed compared to the lightning-fast Manticore.
- <strong>Legacy Connections:</strong> Uses older proprietary USB cables and battery chargers.
