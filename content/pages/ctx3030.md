## The Master of Stability & Identification

## 

### FBS 2 Technology

**Full Band Spectrum 2 (FBS 2)** is the engine that drives the Minelab CTX 3030. Unlike single-frequency detectors that struggle in mineralized ground or saltwater, FBS 2 combines Minelab's multi-frequency transmission (1.5 kHz – 100 kHz) with advanced digital coil-to-detector communication.

The result is significantly better noise cancellation and signal analysis, allowing you to find deep silver coins even in iron-infested sites where other machines go silent.

### The Smart Screen & Target Trace

The full-colour LCD is more than just a display—it's a visual mapping tool:

* **Target Trace:** This feature allows you to see multiple targets simultaneously. You can visually distinguish a good target (like a coin) sitting right next to a bad target (like a nail) based on their position on the FeCo (Ferrous/Conductive) graph.
* **GPS Mapping:** The screen integrates GPS data, allowing you to create Waypoints and view "GeoTrails" so you can see exactly where you have already detected.

***

### 💻 The X-Change 2 Advantage: Easy PC Programming

One of the CTX 3030's most distinct features is its full integration with the **Minelab X-Change 2** PC application.

Unlike newer machines where all settings must be tapped out on a small screen, the CTX 3030 allows you to:

* **Design Custom Profiles on a Large Screen:** Easily drag and drop discrimination patterns and Tone ID profiles using your mouse and keyboard.
* **Manage Your Maps:** View your "Find Points" and "GeoTrails" on Google Maps to analyse where you found treasure.
* **Upload & Download**: Instantly transfer your custom settings to your detector via USB.

> **Note:** This PC-based profile creation facility is **_NOT_** available on the **_Minelab Manticore_**, which relies solely on on-device programming.

### Comparison: Minelab CTX 3030 vs. Minelab Manticore

The [Minelab Manticore](/minelab-manticore/) is faster and lighter, but the CTX 3030 remains the choice for Target ID stability, built-in GPS, and advanced PC customization.

| | Minelab CTX 3030 | Minelab Manticore |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Core technology** | FBS 2 (simultaneous multi-frequency) | Multi-IQ+ (high-power multi-frequency) |
| **PC programming** | X-Change 2 software: build profiles and maps on a PC | No PC profile editor (updates only; programmed on the detector) |
| **Target ID style** | FeCo: 1–50 conductive / 1–35 ferrous | 0–99 conductive scale |
| **Visual display** | Colour LCD with Target Trace | Colour LCD with 2D ID Map |
| **GPS mapping** | Built-in GPS and PC mapping | No GPS |

### ✅ The Pros

* **PC Customization:** Use X-Change 2 to build complex programs easily on your computer rather than fiddling with buttons in the field.
* **Best Visual ID:** Target Trace remains superior for identifying bottle caps versus coins.
* **Stability:** Extremely stable Target IDs at depth; numbers rarely jump on deep silver.
* **GPS:** The only high-end machine that logs your exact find locations.

### ⚠️ The Cons

* **Weight:** It is heavy—a detecting harness (like the Minelab Pro-Swing 45) is often required for long sessions.
* **Recovery Speed:** Slower processor speed compared to the lightning-fast Manticore.
* **Legacy Connections:** Uses older proprietary USB cables and battery chargers.

## Frequently Asked Questions: Minelab CTX 3030

### Is the Minelab CTX 3030 fully waterproof?

Yes, the **Minelab CTX 3030** is completely waterproof to **3 metres (10 feet)**, allowing you to submerge the entire detector for wading in surf, rivers, and lakes. Before taking it underwater, always inspect the main battery O-ring seal for sand or grit and apply a light coat of silicone grease—and remember that the standard wireless module (WM 10) is **_weatherproof_** only, so you will need waterproof headphones plugged directly into the headphone socket for full submersion.

### Is the Minelab CTX 3030 still worth buying?

Yes—if your priority is **deep silver Target ID stability, built-in GPS mapping, and X-Change 2 PC programming**, the CTX 3030 still holds its own in the field. While newer detectors like the [**_Minelab Manticore_**](/minelab-manticore/) are significantly lighter and faster in dense iron carpets, the CTX 3030's FBS 2 engine and Target Trace screen remain unmatched for locking onto deep, high-conductive coins without jumpy ID numbers.

### Which coil is best for the Minelab CTX 3030 on UK pasture vs. beaches?

For general UK pasture and ploughed fields, the stock **CTX 11 (11-inch DD)** offers the best all-round balance of depth and separation, while the **Coiltek 10x5" Elliptical** is the top upgrade for working through heavy iron trash and stubble. For wide-open beaches and clean pasture where you want maximum depth and ground coverage, step up to the **CTX 17 (17x13-inch DD)** or the lighter **Coiltek 14x9" DD**.
